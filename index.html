Questions

The questions everyone asks first.

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front — ever. We work on contingency: our fee is a percentage of what we recover for you, agreed in writing before we begin, and we advance all case costs along the way. If we don't win your case, you owe us nothing at all.

How long will my case take?

Most injury claims resolve in a matter of months; complex cases that go to trial can take a year or more. In your free evaluation we'll give you an honest range for your specific situation — and we never slow-walk a case, because delay only ever helps the insurance company.

What is my case worth?

It depends on your medical costs, lost income, and how the injury changes your daily life — which is exactly why early insurance offers are so often too low. Before you accept any number, let us look at it. The evaluation is free, and you keep every option open.

Do I have to go to court?

Probably not — most of our cases settle without a trial. But insurers pay more when they know your lawyers are genuinely ready for a courtroom, so we prepare every case as if it's going to one. If yours does, we're beside you at every hearing.
